# Terms a new player meets

One plain sentence each (90 characters at most). The same words show in the game as "i" tips, from src/data/help.js.
If you change a line here, change it there too. A test checks they match.

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| Gold | Gold pays for Parts, Crew and upgrades. You earn it from gems and ore. |
| Rig | The Rig is the grid on your bucket. Put Parts on it before each Drop. |
| Drop | Drop lowers your bucket down the well. Go deep, then Haul Up to keep it all. |
| Part | A Part sits on the Rig and does one job, like grabbing gems or guarding the rope. |
| Combo | Parts placed in the right spots boost each other. A Combo shows when they match. |
| Loadout | A Loadout is a saved Rig. Tap a slot to load it. Press and hold to save. |
| Suggest | Suggest fills the Rig with the best Parts you own. You can still change it. |
| Strain | Strain is how tight the rope is. It grows as you go deeper. Full means Snap. |
| Haul Up | Haul Up pulls the bucket up and keeps everything you found. Do it before a Snap. |
| Snap | If the rope Snaps, you keep only 30% of what you found. Haul Up in time. |
| Shards | Shards are a rare coin. Goals and guardians give them. Spend them in the Workshop. |
| Core Dust | Core Dust comes from a Collapse. Each piece adds 10% to all Gold, for good. |
| Collapse | Collapse is a fresh start. You lose Gold, Crew and Parts, and gain Core Dust. |
| Codex | The Codex is your book of finds: gems, Parts, Combos and relics you have seen. |
| Goals | Goals are small jobs for each Drop. Finish them to earn Shards. |
| Crew | Crew earn Gold every second, even when you are away. Buy more to earn faster. |
| Manager | A Manager triples what one kind of Crew earns. Hire one when it is offered. |
| Milestone | Own 10, 25, 50 or more of one Crew and its Gold per second doubles. |
| Workshop | The Workshop sells new Parts and upgrades. Spend your Gold and Shards here. |
| Boon | A Boon is a gift you pick during a Drop. It helps until that Drop ends. |
| Brace | Brace eases the rope and slows the bucket for a moment. Tap it when Strain is high. |
| Surge | Surge makes Gold count double for a moment, but the rope pulls harder. |
| Flare | Flare lights the way and clears the next row of dangers. |
| Guardians | Guardians are big fights deep down. Grab the marked gems to win. |
| Relics | Relics are rare finds. Each gives a small bonus for good. Collect a whole set. |
| Daily reward | Come back each day for free Gold and Shards. A longer streak pays more. |
| Danger | Danger hurts the rope if you do not stop it. Parts that guard keep it safe. |
