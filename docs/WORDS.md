# Words

Tone: calm, confident, a little mysterious. Short sentences. Everyday words.

## Words we use on screen
Gold, Shards, Core Dust, Rig, Part, Drop, Haul Up, Snap, Strain, Depth, Biome, Crew, Workshop, Collapse, Codex, Goals, Combo, Loadout, Gem, Ore, Danger, Step.

- **Gem**: a stone that rises past the bucket. Worth more the better it is.
- **Ore**: rough rock that rises past the bucket. Worth less than a gem.
- **Danger**: something that hurts the rope if you do not stop it (a rock, a bat, a root). Never say "hazard".
- **Step**: one small move forward, such as one tutorial step. Never say "beat" or "tick".

## Say this, not that
| Not this | Say this |
|---|---|
| hazard | danger |
| lane | column |
| item | gem or ore (or "thing" if both) |
| tier (on screen) | level, for example "one level better" |
| beat | step |

DECIDED by the Manager: players see "level", never "tier" (and "danger", never "hazard"). Developer files and code may keep the word tier.

## Words we never show
beat, lane, tick, entity, null, undefined, NaN, Infinity, seed, slice, bus, payload, debug.

## Relic Wall and Looks
Words: Relic Wall (also just "the Wall"), Look, Looks, Wear, Worn, Locked, New.

- **Relic Wall**: the page that shows every relic you have found. Hidden ones show "???" and a short hint. Never say "relic book", "slice" or "record".
- **Look**: a colour style for the Bucket, the Rope or the Well. A Look changes only how things look. Say "Look", never "skin" or "cosmetic".
- **Wear**: put a Look on. A Look you wear says "Worn". Never say "equip".
- **Wall bonus**: a small bonus for finding many relics (10, 20 and 36 found). Say "+1% Gold" or "+1% better gems". Never say "buff".
- Hints for hidden relics: "Hides in the Clay Biome" (and the other five Biomes).
- Ways to get a Look: "Find 6 relics", "Earn an achievement", "Buy with Shards", "Free".
- Counts read "12 of 36 found".

## Contracts, Mastery, Daily Dive (HOOK-2)
- **Contracts**: three small jobs at a time, for example "Collect 20 gems in one Drop". Finish one, claim it, and a new one appears. Fresh ones every day.
- **Mastery**: a Part gets better the more you use it. Five levels, written "Mastery 3 of 5". A small row of 5 diamonds shows it. Never say "XP", "tier" or "proficiency".
- **Daily Dive**: one special Drop each day, the same for everyone. One free try, your best Depth for the day, a streak in days, and a chest at the end. "Today's twist" is the rule that changes that day.
- Chest names: Small chest, Good chest, Rich chest.
