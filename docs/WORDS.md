# Words

Tone: calm, confident, a little mysterious. Short sentences. Everyday words.

Gold, Shards, Core Dust, Rig, Part, Drop, Haul Up, Snap, Strain, Depth, Biome, Crew, Workshop, Collapse, Codex, Goals, Combo, Loadout.
