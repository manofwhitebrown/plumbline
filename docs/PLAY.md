# How to play Plumbline

Lower a bucket down a bottomless well. Grab what rises past it. Go deeper than last time. One more meter.

## Where to play

- **Laptop:** open https://manofwhitebrown.github.io/plumbline/ . The game is a tall column in the middle. That is on purpose. **Space** = Drop or Haul Up, **R** = Rig, **Esc** = back.
- **Phone:** install the Android app (ask the team for the newest file). It works without internet.

**Page looks old?** Hard refresh: **Ctrl + Shift + R** (Mac: **Cmd + Shift + R**).

## Install on Android

Ask the team for the newest Android file and open it on your phone. Steps for the team (test and signed builds) are in docs/onboard/DEV_TESTING.md and docs/ANDROID.md.

## One round

1. **Rig.** Put your Parts on the grid. Each Part has one job, like grabbing gems or guarding the rope. Tap **Suggest** and the game fills the grid for you.
2. **Drop.** The bucket sinks. Gems and ore rise past it. Your Parts grab them and turn them into Gold.
3. **Watch Strain.** The rope gets tighter the deeper you go. Dangers hurt it too.
4. **Haul Up** to keep everything you found. If the rope **Snaps** first, you keep only 30%.
5. **Spend.** Buy Parts and upgrades in the **Workshop**. Hire **Crew** who earn Gold even while you are away.

## What the buttons mean

- **Crew:** helpers who earn Gold every second. Own 10, 25 or 50 of one kind and it doubles. A **Manager** triples it.
- **Workshop:** buy new Parts and upgrades with Gold and Shards.
- **Loadout:** a saved Rig. Tap a slot to load it. Press and hold to save.
- **Suggest:** fills the Rig with the best Parts you own.
- **Combo:** Parts in the right spots boost each other.
- **Codex:** your book of finds.
- **Goals:** small jobs for each Drop. They pay Shards.
- **Contracts:** three small jobs at a time, like "Collect 20 gems in one Drop". Claim one and a new one appears.
- **Daily Dive:** one special Drop each day, the same for everyone. One free try, a streak in days, and a chest.
- **Mastery:** a Part gets better the more you use it. Five levels, shown as five diamonds.
- **Relic Wall:** every relic you have found. Finding many gives small bonuses.
- **Looks:** colour styles for the Bucket, Rope or Well. They change only how things look. Tap to **Wear** one.
- **Daily reward:** free Gold and Shards each day. Tap the gift on Home.
- **Collapse:** a fresh start. You lose Gold, Crew and Parts. You gain **Core Dust**, and every piece adds 10% to all Gold for good.

## Words you will meet

- **Shards:** a rare coin from Goals and guardians.
- **Boon:** a gift you pick during a Drop. It lasts until the Drop ends.
- **Brace:** eases the rope and slows the bucket. **Surge:** double Gold for a moment, but the rope pulls harder. **Flare:** clears the next row of dangers.
- **Guardians:** big fights deep down. Grab the marked gems to win.
- **Relics:** rare finds with small, lasting bonuses.

## Tips

- Not sure what to do? Home shows one short line under the Gold number: **what to do next**.
- Tap the little **i** next to a title for a short explanation. Tap anywhere to close it.
- Every word is listed in docs/onboard/TERMS.md. The game also has a **How to play** page.

Settings has **Reduce motion** and **Large text** if you need them.
